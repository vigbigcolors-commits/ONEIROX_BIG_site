REM cortex & EEG

On the trace, REM cortex can look almost awake: fast, low-amplitude rhythms. Offline sensory gates stay shut, so that activation turns inward — rewriting traces instead of navigating the room.

What “wake-like” actually means

Beta- and gamma-range chatter during REM is not proof you are conscious in the daylight sense. It means local circuits are metabolically hot: binding fragments, testing emotional predictions, discarding weak synapses. The dream you remember is a compressed side-effect of that pass — not a moral parable downloaded from a dictionary.

Consolidation vs threat rehearsal

When hippocampal–neocortical dialogue dominates, nights feel like filing and re-linking. When amygdala bias spikes, nights feel like pursuit, shame, or unfinished confrontation. Decode’s SIGNAL line is trying to name which of those machines was louder — not which animal “means” what.

Pruning is not poetry

Synaptic downscaling overnight frees energy for tomorrow’s learning. Odd, compressed, or “nonsensical” scenes often mark aggressive pruning + remix, not a coded message you failed to crack.

How to read a wake-like trace without turning it into a sermon

This page is about REM cortex, not N3 delta. Fast low-amplitude EEG in REM is metabolic heat in networks that are offline from the room. Hippocampal–neocortical dialogue files.
