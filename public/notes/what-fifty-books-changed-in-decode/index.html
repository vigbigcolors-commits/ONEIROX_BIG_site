Lab Note 03 · Research into product

What fifty books actually changed in Lab Search

Vigen G.R. 15 Aug 2026 · Oneirox Lab Notes

People ask for the reading list as if the number itself were the product. Fifty books is not a flex. It is the time it took to stop believing that a better dictionary would fix dream interpretation.

I read clinicians and skeptics. I read Walker on REM and emotional memory. Cartwright on dream continuity. Damasio on somatic markers — the body tagging significance before the cortex narrates. Revonsuo on threat simulation. LeDoux on fast fear pathways. Solms on images generated to explain states already active. Porges on social threat as physiology. Van der Kolk on the body keeping score. Sapolsky on stress and consolidation. And yes, Jung — not to resurrect archetype tables, but to understand why the mind reaches for metaphor when mechanism would be more honest.

The books did not give me five meanings. They forced me to commit to one format.

SIGNAL · BODY · MORNING

That is the concrete product change. Not a longer essay. A structure:

SIGNAL — the core truth in one sentence: what the brain was doing.
BODY — the active mechanism in plain language.
MORNING — the question the nervous system was trying to deliver to waking-you.

Fourteen research frameworks sit behind that format. They are not a citation wall at the bottom of a page. They are the decision tree that refuses “snake = betrayal OR transformation OR wisdom — pick one you like.”

Why Mapper exists because of Damasio

Interoception is not optional decoration. If the body often knows first, then a decoder that never asks about temperature, pressure, weight, or resistance across zones is incomplete by design. That is why the Sensory Dream Mapper exists — and why it can feed Decode.

What I still refuse to claim

I am not a university lab, not a psychiatrist, not a therapist. Decode is a research-informed instrument. Persistent distress belongs with a licensed professional. That honesty is part of the methodology — see About and Methodology.

The Lab keeps growing with free instruments so Decode stays rare and honest. The books were the spine. The instruments are the proof of work.
