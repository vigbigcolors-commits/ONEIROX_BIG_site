Why N1 gets its own hub

N1 is the theta gate into sleep (4–7 Hz). The body still reports falling, hypnic jerks, limb-float, and onset breathing pauses — residue of the wake-to-sleep switch, not a dream plot. This hub is not a blog about “N1 dreams.” It is the index of 4 high-density metric utilities currently allowed to crawl. Each card is a different symptom × N1 × context triple. Same phase, different hardware. Mixing N1/N2/N3/REM into one omen paragraph is how thin pages get born; this URL refuses that mix.

Contexts present in the live N1 index: onset. Symptom families: Exploding-head sensory burst (physiological), Sleep-onset breathing pause (physiological), Hypnic myoclonus (sleep start), Tachycardia in hypnagogia. If two links look similar, read the context suffix — onset is not awakening; fragmentation is not mid-cycle. Cortical language for this stage stays inside N1 physiology, not a personality metaphor.

This N1 hub is intentionally small. Dense-gate is stricter than “cover the keyword.” Right now 4 rows pass. Exploding-head sensory burst (physiological) in N1 sleep (onset) is the live example: use it if the waking body matches; do not pad the hub with thin cousins. When more N1 rows earn markers + context + a 17-character-class bind (for somatic: example-complete fields), they appear here via drip, not by hand.

Slow-wave / N1 nights are easy to misread as “nothing happened” because recall is poor. That is why a single motor-residue row still deserves a hub: confusional arousal leftover, stillness, or N3-exit jerks are stage-shift hardware. If your night was vivid story, you were probably not in N1 for the remembered scene — check REM or N2 hubs instead of forcing this URL.

How to work a night that belongs on this hub: 1) write the first twenty seconds of residue (heat, weight, mute, twitch, jaw, breath). 2) pick the N1 card whose title names that residue. 3) read that row’s gauges and markers — numbers are URL-specific. 4) carry the row into Lab Search; do not paste a symbol. 5) if the night was mostly plot, use Dream Meaning; if it was mostly body, stay in /somatic.

N1 is a sleep-architecture label, not a type of person. N1 is a theta gate into sleep, N2 a sigma/spindle window, N3 a delta well, REM a dreaming state with spinal atonia. This page only lists N1. Neighbor hubs: N1, N2, N3, REM.

What this hub will not do: diagnose apnea, REM sleep behavior disorder, or psychiatric disease. Persistent dream enactment, choking, or daytime collapse needs a clinician. What it will do: keep N1 metrics honest so Decode is not guessing from a plot summary. Methodology lives on About; Lab Notes by Vigen sit at /notes.
