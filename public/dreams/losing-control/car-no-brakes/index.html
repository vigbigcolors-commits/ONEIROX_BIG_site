Dream About a Car with No Brakes

Pedal to the floor on the wrong pedal. Speed rises. Stopping is not in the instrument set tonight.

SIGNAL

Agency with failed inhibitory control — a machine avatar for “I cannot decelerate this trajectory.”

What's actually happening

Vehicles are practiced control interfaces. Dreams corrupt brakes when waking life has acceleration without off-ramps: escalation, addiction dynamics, runaway projects.

Unlike elevator drops (passive), no-brakes often keep you in the driver’s seat — responsible yet ineffective.

Real driving stress and near-misses bias continuity.
