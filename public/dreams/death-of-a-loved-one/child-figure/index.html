Dream About a Child Dying

A child — yours or a stand-in — is in lethal danger or gone. Protective circuitry does not do mild.

SIGNAL

Caregiving and protective systems are running a worst-case loss simulation — high amygdala, high urgency.

What's actually happening

These dreams devastate dreamers and still are usually anxiety products, not forecasts. Frequency plus daytime impairment is when to seek support.

New parents and pregnancy periods elevate odds via protective hypervigilance.

Unknown children can stand for projects, inner-child material, or general vulnerability — still start with caregiving load.
