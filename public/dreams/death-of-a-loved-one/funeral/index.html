Grief with witnesses

Dream About a Funeral

Casket, clothes, speeches. Loss becomes public procedure. The dream is processing grief as a social event.

SIGNAL

Bereavement systems plus social ritual schemas are consolidating — how loss is performed in front of others.

What's actually happening

Funerals in dreams appear after real deaths and also when a role/identity is ending and needs a rite.

Wrong funeral details (laughing guests, empty casket) mark prediction error in how mourning “should” go.

Being unprepared for the funeral stacks evaluation onto grief.
