Vivid Snake Dream at Awakening

The snake is hyper-real as you wake — color, scale, presence extreme. That intensity is REM visual/affective salience, not an omen chart about hue.

SIGNAL

Phasic REM imagery with high visual salience overlaps the wake edge — hypnopompic continuation of a predator schema.

What's actually happening

Vividness tracks arousal and sensory gating at transitions, not moral meanings of green/black/white. Oneirox refuses color-as-symbol doorways; intensity is the variable.

Hypnopompic carryover can make the snake feel briefly “still in the room.” That is edge-state perception, then it collapses.

If vivid animal imagery at wake is frequent with paralysis or fear, read sleep-paralysis pages in parallel.
