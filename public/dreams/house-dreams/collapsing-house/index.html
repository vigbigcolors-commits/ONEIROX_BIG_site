Scaffold fails

Dream About a House Collapsing

Beams crack. Floors tilt. The container that held your rooms gives way. Structure-under-load is the story.

SIGNAL

A stable container schema (house-as-structure) is failing under stress — common when life scaffolding feels unreliable.

What's actually happening

Collapse differs from flood: the medium is not overwhelm volume but integrity loss. Supports fail.

Moves, breakups, job loss, and health scares all destabilize scaffolds and bias this imagery.

If you escape before total collapse, agency remains; if you are buried, overwhelm won the night’s math.

By scenario

Agentic destruction — change you are initiating, with fear of fallout.
