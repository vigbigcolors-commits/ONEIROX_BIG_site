Approach vs loyalty

Dream About Cheating on Your Partner

You cross a line in the dream. Guilt arrives on schedule or late. Desire and loyalty systems in conflict.

SIGNAL

Approach motivation toward a non-bond target conflicts with pair-bond loyalty tagging — REM rehearses the collision.

What's actually happening

These dreams do not equal intent. They often process attraction, neglect, novelty hunger, or fear of being the betrayer.

If guilt dominates, loyalty systems are strong. If thrill dominates without guilt, ask about numbness or exit fantasy — carefully, waking.

Unknown dream partners often mean the conflict is thematic more than about a specific person.
