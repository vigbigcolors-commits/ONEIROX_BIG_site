Dream About Passport or Border Trouble

Lost passport. Wrong stamp. Queue that never ends. For migrants and their children, documents are limbic objects.

SIGNAL

Identity-authorization tokens are under threat in the dream graph — access failure with legal costume, high diaspora relevance.

What's actually happening

Wallets and badges are access tokens. Passports are louder: they decide who crosses. Diaspora families carry intergenerational vigilance about papers. REM uses that vigilance as plot when belonging or travel stress is active.

This is cousin to lost-wallet LF pages, with higher identity stakes. Not a prediction that ICE or a border agent is coming tomorrow — an overnight rehearsal of document anxiety.

If you wake checking a real passport drawer, the continuity loop is already obvious. Reduce waking chaos around renewals when you can.
