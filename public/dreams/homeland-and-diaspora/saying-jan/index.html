Dream About Saying Jan

Someone calls you jan. Or you say it and wake aching. Jan is not just vocabulary — it is a kinship particle English does not own.

SIGNAL

An untranslatable affection-particle speech act is active in REM — heritage pragmatics consolidating belonging more tightly than generic “I love you.”

What's actually happening

Untranslatable particles are high-value Lab material. Jan compresses care, softness, and address into one syllable Armenian speakers feel in the chest. Dreams that hinge on jan are often kinship-speech consolidations — especially after English-only days.

This sits next to speaking-Armenian pages but is narrower: one pragmatic particle as the whole emotional payload. Mechanism: heritage speech act as affiliation effector.

Wrong person saying jan, or jan said coldly, is affiliation mismatch — the particle’s warmth inverted.
