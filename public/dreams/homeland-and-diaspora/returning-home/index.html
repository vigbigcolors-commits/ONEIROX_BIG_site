What's actually happening

Return dreams are common in diaspora psychology research as continuity of unfinished approach: visits planned, visits delayed, guilt about not visiting, excitement before a ticket. REM stages the approach whether or not a flight exists.

Smooth arrival vs endless transit sorts whether the night’s affective math allowed completion. Lost luggage and wrong terminals are access friction layered on approach.

This is not a command to move permanently. It is overnight processing of approach desire.
