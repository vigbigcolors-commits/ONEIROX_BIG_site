Ascent as effort narrative

Dream About Climbing Mount Ararat

Switchbacks. Thin air. The summit stays ahead. This is effort geometry wearing a homeland mountain — not a prophecy about your destiny.

SIGNAL

Goal-directed effort is being simulated on the highest-salience homeland peak available — motor intention plus attachment destination.

What's actually happening

Climb dreams elsewhere use random mountains. Using Ararat stacks homeland destination onto effort. Continuity: a hard goal that feels like “getting back” or “proving belonging.”

Heavy legs on the climb still read as REM atonia throttle — same hardware as chase dreams, different costume.

Summit reached vs never reached sorts completion affect. Weather that stops you is external-blocker framing for the same effort loop.
