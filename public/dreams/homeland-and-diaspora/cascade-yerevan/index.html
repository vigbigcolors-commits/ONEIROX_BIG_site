Dream About the Cascade in Yerevan

Endless steps, museums in the stone, the city opening below. Cascade dreams are effort + orientation on a named Yerevan landmark.

SIGNAL

A landmark-ascent schema (Cascade) pairs motor effort with capital-city orientation during REM consolidation.

What's actually happening

Generic stair dreams often track effort or status. Cascade is specific: a Yerevan landmark that literally stages ascent toward a viewpoint (and historically toward Ararat sightlines). Mechanism: named landmark + motor climb + city orientation.

Exhaustion mid-Cascade is effort cost on homeland approach. Flying up the steps is agency fantasy on the same map.

Distinct from climbing-Ararat: here the object is civic architecture, not the peak itself.

By scenario

You reach the top and see Ararat

Cascade ascent completing into peak place bind — two schemas stacked.

Steps broken or endless

Approach blocked — effort without arrival payoff.
