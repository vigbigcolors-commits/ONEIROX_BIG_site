Smell and sacred place

Dream About Armenian Church or Incense

Incense. Chant. Candle heat. The body remembers church before the plot explains itself.

SIGNAL

Olfactory and sacred-place schemas are co-activated in REM — high-salience sensory belonging, common after holidays or grief.

What's actually happening

Smell is tightly wired to memory and emotion. Church incense is a practiced cue for many Armenian families. REM can reload the cue with place and people attached — mechanism first, miracle optional and unverified.

Badarak fragments, choir, and stone coolness are multimodal binds. If peace dominates, low threat dye. If panic in the aisle, evaluation or grief may be stacked on the sacred map.

Oneirox will not tell you a saint visited. We will tell you the sensory-attachment loop is real and testable against your waking calendar (Soorp days, funerals, weddings).

By scenario

Sacred place without congregation — solitude inside belonging architecture.

Incense only, no building
