Bread as kinship material

Dream About Lavash

Thin bread, tonir heat, hands passing sheets. Lavash is staple sensory kinship — denser than generic “food dream.”

SIGNAL

A staple-food kinship sensory schema (lavash / tonir heat) is consolidating — oral and thermal cues under affiliation drive.

What's actually happening

Family-table pages track feast affiliation. Lavash narrows to the staple: the bread that appears at almost every Armenian table and carries making-by-hand memory. REM uses high-frequency sensory objects when belonging consolidates.

Burned lavash, empty tonir, or bread that tears wrong is hospitality competence threat. Sharing warm lavash is affiliation reward.

Refuse omen logic (“bread means wealth”). Mechanism: staple sensory + kinship script.

By scenario

Baking lavash with elders

Intergenerational skill + thermal sensory — strong elder bind.

Lavash everywhere but you cannot eat

Affiliation object without oral payoff — frustration on a kinship cue.
