Tongue as identity tool

Dream About Speaking Armenian

Words you half-know. Or perfect fluency you lack waking. Language is an identity effector channel.

SIGNAL

Speech-motor and language-identity maps are active in REM — belonging negotiated through the tongue, not through a symbol dictionary.

What's actually happening

Heritage speakers and learners carry language shame and pride in the same jaw. Dreams of fluent Armenian often appear when identity desire is high; dreams of failing Armenian often appear after a real conversation stumble or family comment.

Related to tongue-stuck and mute-scream pages when the channel locks — here the content is specifically heritage language.

Suddenly understanding grandparents in-dream is affiliation reward via language bind. Being laughed at for accent is evaluation threat on the same effector.

By scenario
