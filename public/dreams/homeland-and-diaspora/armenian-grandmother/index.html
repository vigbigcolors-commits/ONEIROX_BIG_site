What's actually happening

In many Armenian families the grandmother (or great-aunt) is a dense node: recipes, church, first language, migration stories. REM reloads that node when belonging, grief, or duty is active — whether she is living or deceased.

Alive-and-well grandmother dreams are often continuity of relationship. Deceased-grandmother dreams are often grief reconsolidation or comfort seeking — still mechanism, not proof of visitation. Treat distress seriously if it impairs mornings.
