The capital as a full place file

Dream About Being in Yerevan

Not a postcard peak — the city: heat on stone, traffic noise, a street you almost name. Diaspora nights often cast Yerevan as the densest urban homeland schema available.

SIGNAL

An episodic or culturally trained capital-city place map is consolidating in REM — self-location inside Yerevan, not a generic “Armenia” category load.

What's actually happening

“I was in Yerevan” is one of the highest-salience search sentences for Armenian-American dreamers. Sleep science reads it as place-file replay: grid, landmarks, language on signs, the feeling of being inside the map. Continuity hypothesis: unfinished belonging or travel plans hire the most detailed city schema you own.

If you have walked Northern Avenue or Republic Square waking, episodic memory can reload with high fidelity. If you know the city only from relatives’ videos, cultural training still supplies a usable map — cheaper for REM than inventing a neutral city.

Spiritual blogs rarely own this query with mechanism. Oneirox does: city place-map + attachment, not a fortune about your year.
