Dream About a Crowd Staring at You

Hundreds of faces turned. Silence or murmur. You are the object. Evaluation without a plot beyond visibility.

SIGNAL

Population-scale gaze binds social-evaluation systems at high intensity without requiring chase or exam scripts.

What's actually happening

Staring crowds are efficient: no need for dialogue. The threat is rank and exposure.

Stage fright, viral attention, and public speaking load bias continuity.

If you stare back and the crowd breaks, agency reclaim; if freeze, monitoring mode won.
