Dream About Losing Your Wallet

Pocket empty. Cards, ID, cash — the compact self-and-resource kit missing. Panic is proportional to how much modern life fits in a wallet.

SIGNAL

A portable identity/resource token is lost in the dream graph — access failure more than abstract poverty symbolism.

What's actually happening

Wallets carry authorization. Losing them stages “I cannot prove or pay.” That is access threat.

Phone-lost dreams are cousins in the smartphone era; wallets remain strong for older continuity templates.

Searching endlessly without finding is open-loop anxiety; finding it empty is resource-zero with identity intact.
