Dream About a Wedding Going Wrong

The dress doesn't fit, the venue is wrong, you're late, or the wrong person shows up at the altar. Wedding-disaster dreams are one of the most reliably misread dreams there is.

SIGNAL

Because weddings combine commitment, planning, social attention, and emotion, they are rich dream material; a disaster plot is not evidence that the relationship is wrong.

What's actually happening

Major events often enter dreams because they dominate attention and emotion while awake. Weddings are especially dense: relationships, family, logistics, performance, money, identity, and public visibility can all be active at once.

That makes anxiety-themed wedding dreams unsurprising before or after major relationship events, but it does not justify reading them as predictions or as proof of hidden doubt. The most defensible interpretation starts with whatever waking concern was already present.

Evidence boundary: research does not support a one-to-one translation from a dream theme to a single waking-life cause. Use the physiology and continuity evidence as context, not diagnosis.
