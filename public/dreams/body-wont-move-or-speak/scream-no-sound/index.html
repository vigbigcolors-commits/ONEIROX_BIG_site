Dream About Screaming with No Sound

You scream. Air moves. No voice. Horror films stole this from REM physiology.

SIGNAL

Intended vocal alarm meets REM motor inhibition — the narrative becomes mute screaming rather than successful call for help.

What's actually happening

This can occur inside chase/attack dreams or alone. It is the same vocal lock family as mute sleep paralysis, sometimes without full body paralysis awareness.

Panic rises because social rescue is unavailable — help-seeking channel closed.

Waking with a sore throat is uncommon but possible after intense attempted phonation.
