Dream About Legs Too Heavy to Move

You command walk. Legs answer with sludge. Not always a chase — sometimes just trying to cross a room.

SIGNAL

Locomotor intention under REM atonia is experienced as mass, mud, or power failure in the limbs.

What's actually happening

Heavy legs appear in chase dreams often, but also in neutral scenes: late for nothing, just unable. That isolates the motor signature from threat plot.

Illness days, hard workouts, and real fatigue bias continuity via limb interoception.

Distinguish from sleep paralysis: if you know you are awake in bed, that is a different edge state.
