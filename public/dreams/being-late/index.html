Time genuinely works differently while you dream

Dream About Being Late or Time Moving Wrong

You're already late and getting later, or a clock is visibly moving the wrong way, or a whole day compresses into what feels like minutes. This isn't a metaphor stretch — dream cognition really does distort time.

SIGNAL

Dream time can feel compressed, stretched, or inconsistent; a being-late dream may connect with waking time pressure, but no single REM timing mechanism explains the theme.

What's actually happening

Dream narratives often contain discontinuities in sequence, duration, place, and causality. Research does not provide a simple neural clock rule that lets us translate a backward clock or missed deadline into a specific REM mechanism.

Being late is also a familiar waking concern, so continuity with deadlines, travel, obligations, or a general sense of time pressure is plausible. That interpretation should be tested against current life rather than assumed from the imagery.

Evidence boundary: research does not support a one-to-one translation from a dream theme to a single waking-life cause. Use the physiology and continuity evidence as context, not diagnosis.
