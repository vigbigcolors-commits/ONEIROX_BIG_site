What's actually happening

Flights are cultural icons of non-negotiable departure. Dreams use them when waking deadlines feel similarly final.

Airport navigation failure often stacks with lateness — wrong-room exam’s cousin in travel costume.

Real upcoming travel biases continuity; so does any “if I miss this, I cannot recover” work event.
