Dream About Being Late to a Wedding

Dress half-on. Roads wrong. Ceremony starting. You are late to a bond ritual — social time, not just clock time.

SIGNAL

Time-threat attached to a ceremonial belonging event — evaluation by a community watching a timed rite.

What's actually happening

Weddings concentrate social eyes and irreversible timing. Missing them in dreams marks fear of failing a relational role on schedule.

Even if no wedding is planned, promotions, launches, and family rites of passage can cast in wedding costume.

If you are the one marrying, lateness may encode ambivalence; if a guest, belonging anxiety.
