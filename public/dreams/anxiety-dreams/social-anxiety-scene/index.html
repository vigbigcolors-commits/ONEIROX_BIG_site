Dream About a Social Anxiety Scene

A party. A greeting gone wrong. Faces wait. Social anxiety does not need a monster; it needs an audience.

SIGNAL

Social-evaluation threat is staged as interpersonal performance failure — continuity with waking social fear.

What's actually happening

Blushing, blanking, saying the wrong thing, and being ignored are common props. They are competence/belonging threats in social costume.

Upcoming events prime these heavily the nights before.

Successful social moments in the same dream mark coping rehearsal beginning to update.
