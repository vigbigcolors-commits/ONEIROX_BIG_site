Work with no gain

Dream About Swimming Against the Current

You kick hard. The shore does not arrive. Effort is high; displacement is near zero. That ratio is the mechanism.

SIGNAL

High intended motor effort meeting a resisting field — often atonia-throttled progress narrativized as current, not laziness prophecy.

What's actually happening

Like heavy-leg chase, against-current swimming is a progress-fail story. Water provides a continuous resisting medium the cortex can feel as physics.

Deadline treadmill weeks and caregiving grind bias continuity here: waking effort that does not move the needle.

If you suddenly ride with the current, coping reframes mid-REM — note what changed in the dream’s rules.

By scenario

Directed river resistance vs chaotic ocean — different control stories, same effort-fail family.
