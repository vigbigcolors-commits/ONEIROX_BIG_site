SIGNAL

What's actually happening

Unlike ocean tidal waves, house floods personalize overwhelm: the load is inside your mapped safe space.

Continuity often tracks household stress, financial leak metaphors people already use waking, or actual leak/plumbing worry — start with real water events before symbolism.

Rising water that you can still walk in differs from sudden submersion — tempo sorts chronic load from acute shock.
