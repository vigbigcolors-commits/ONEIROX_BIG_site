Dream About Failing a Job Interview

Blank mind. Wrong answers. Interviewers unimpressed. You needed access; the gate scored you out.

SIGNAL

High-stakes entry evaluation with competence threat — cousin to exam dreams in hiring costume.

What's actually happening

Interviews are modern exams with livelihood attached. Dreams reuse exam hardware freely.

Active job searches bias this heavily; so does fear of being “re-interviewed” by life after a mistake at work.

If you sabotage yourself on purpose in the dream, ambivalence about the role may be present.
